## Illustrations

I'm hoping to have one illustration per newsletter. Not always an editorial image about a specific topic. Might be a bird sketch, or an urban sketch. But I'm planning to stay on point with most of the sketches. Like the one on this page. I used it to illustrate my sengment "who are you choosing to exclude?".

It is an ink and watercolor sketch of two people who can't use stairs, in front of an elevator closed for "exercise week"
